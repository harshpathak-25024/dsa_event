--- HOW TO PLAY ---

GAME 1: BLOCKLY MAZE

Snap programming blocks together to guide your character to the red target. You must solve the puzzle using the limited blocks provided!

  • Move Forward: Steps your character forward exactly one space in the direction they are facing.
  • Turn Left/Right: Rotates your character 90 degrees without moving forward.
  • Repeat Until: A loop! Any blocks placed inside this "C" shaped block will repeat continuously until you reach the finish line.
  • If Path: A condition. It checks if there is a path in a specific direction. If true, it runs the blocks inside it.

GAME 2: UNTANGLE

You will see a messy web of points connected by lines. Your goal is to drag the points around the screen until ZERO lines intersect.

  • Lines that cross each other will be dark/red.
  • When a line is completely free of intersections, it turns green or fades out.
  • The puzzle is solved when the entire shape is untangled!
