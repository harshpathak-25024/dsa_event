--- HOW TO PLAY ---

GAME 1: BLOCKLY MAZE

Snap programming blocks together to guide your character to the red target. You must solve the puzzle using the limited blocks provided!

  • Move Forward: Steps your character forward exactly one space.
  • Turn Left/Right: Rotates your character 90 degrees.
  • Repeat Until: A loop! Blocks inside will repeat until you reach the finish.
  • If Path: A condition. It checks if there is a path in a specific direction.

GAME 2: UNTANGLE

You will see a messy web of points. Drag them until ZERO lines intersect.

  • Lines that cross each other will be dark/red.
  • When a line is free of intersections, it turns green or fades out.
  • The puzzle is solved when the entire shape is untangled!
